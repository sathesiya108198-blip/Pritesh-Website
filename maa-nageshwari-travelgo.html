Who we are

ℹ About Us

Maa Nageshwari Tours and Travels is owned and operated by Pritesh Rajput, serving travellers across Gujarat and Rajasthan with a daily AC Sleeper coach between Surat and Jaipur. Our buses run every night with 36 comfortable sleeper sofas across lower and upper decks.
